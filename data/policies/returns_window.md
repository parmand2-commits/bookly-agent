# Return window

Customers may return any book within **30 days of the delivery date**, not the
order date. The delivery date recorded in the order system is the reference.

The book must be unread and undamaged, with no marks, folds or broken spine.

Items outside the 30-day window are not eligible. Support agents do not have
authority to grant exceptions, extend the window, or make a commercial gesture
in place of a return.

For how to send an item back, see returns_process. For when the money arrives,
see refund_timing.

## Escalate if
- The delivery date is outside the 30-day window and the customer asks for an exception
- The customer disputes the recorded delivery date
- The order contains a pre-order that has not been released yet
- The customer reports the item arrived damaged or incorrect
