# Damaged or incorrect items

When a customer reports that a book arrived damaged, torn, missing pages, or is a different edition or title than ordered, this is treated as a Bookly error, not a standard return. Do not route it through returns_process, and do not charge the customer for return postage.

These cases do not have a fixed resolution path for support agents to apply on their own. Every report of a damaged or incorrect item is escalated to the returns exceptions team, who confirm the fault, arrange the return at no cost to the customer, and decide on replacement versus refund.

The delivery date used to check whether the order still falls within the standard return window is defined in returns_window. That window is not extended for damaged or incorrect items, but items reported after the window still require escalation rather than automatic refusal.

Ask the customer for a description or photo of the fault before escalating, if the channel allows it.

## Escalate if
- The customer reports the item arrived damaged, incomplete, or is the wrong title or edition
- The customer requests a replacement instead of a refund
- The reported fault cannot be confirmed from the customer's description alone
- The order falls outside the window defined in returns_window
