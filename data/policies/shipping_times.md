# Delivery estimates

Published transit estimates, counted in business days from dispatch, are: **France 2-4 days, Belgium and Germany 3-6 days, and the rest of the EU 5-9 days**. These are estimates, not guarantees, and exclude any time an order spends unshipped before dispatch.

Agents should quote the upper bound of the relevant zone when a customer asks how soon an order will arrive, and should always check the order's actual dispatch date in the order system rather than the order date — an order placed today does not start its transit clock until it ships.

These estimates are the reference point used to determine whether an order counts as late; see delayed_orders for the threshold and what Bookly does once it is reached.

Estimates do not account for customs delays outside mainland EU zones or carrier disruptions communicated separately.

## Escalate if
- The customer's delivery zone is not covered by the ranges in this file
- The order has not dispatched and the customer needs a binding delivery date
- Tracking shows movement inconsistent with the expected zone
- The order already qualifies as late under delayed_orders
