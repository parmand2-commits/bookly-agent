# Pre-orders

A pre-order is **charged when the title ships, not when the order is placed**. No payment is taken at checkout beyond an authorization hold that some card issuers may display; that hold is not a charge.

If the publisher moves the release date, the pre-order stays open at the new date automatically. The customer is not charged anything because of the delay, and no action is required from support beyond confirming the new date in the order system if asked.

A pre-order can be cancelled under the same status rule as any other order — see order_cancellation — and since it has not shipped, cancelling it means no charge is ever made.

Once a pre-ordered title ships and is delivered, the standard return window applies from the delivery date; see returns_window for that window.

## Escalate if
- The customer was charged before the title shipped
- The release date has moved more than once for the same title
- The customer wants a refund for a pre-order that has already shipped
- The pre-order does not appear in the order system despite a confirmation email
