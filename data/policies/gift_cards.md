# Gift cards

Bookly gift cards are valid for **24 months from the date of purchase or issue**. Once that period passes, the remaining balance expires and cannot be reinstated or reissued by support agents.

Gift cards are **not refundable for cash** under any circumstance, including an unused or partially used balance. A gift card can be combined with one promotional discount code in the same order, and more than one gift card can be applied to a single order at checkout.

A gift card applied to an order still counts toward the order subtotal used for the free-delivery threshold; see shipping_costs for that threshold.

If a customer reports a gift card code that does not work, check the balance and expiry in the account system before assuming the code itself is invalid — a zero balance and an expired code produce the same error message to the customer.

A gift card cannot be exchanged for a different gift card or transferred between accounts by support agents.

## Escalate if
- The gift card shows a balance but fails to apply at checkout
- The customer disputes the recorded expiry or issue date
- The customer requests a cash refund for a gift card balance
- A gift card appears to have been used without the holder's authorization
