# Delivery charges

Standard delivery is free on orders of **35 EUR or more**, based on the order subtotal at checkout before any discount is deducted. Below that threshold, delivery is charged at **4.90 EUR to France, 5.90 EUR to Belgium, and 6.50 EUR to Germany**.

These are the standard rates for the zones covered; delivery pricing for other EU destinations is confirmed at checkout and is not listed here. If a customer asks why they were charged for delivery, check the order subtotal against the threshold in this file rather than the current cart — a promotion applied after checkout does not retroactively change a charge already made.

Delivery charges are separate from the transit estimates in shipping_times and are not affected by how late an order runs.

Support agents cannot waive a delivery charge as a goodwill gesture without escalation.

## Escalate if
- The customer's subtotal was above the free-delivery threshold but was still charged
- The customer requests a delivery charge be waived or refunded
- The zone is not one of the standard rates listed in this file
- A promotion appears to have been miscalculated at checkout
